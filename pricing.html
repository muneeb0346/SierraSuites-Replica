Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our plans

Can I change plans later?

Absolutely! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Your subscription will be prorated based on the remaining days in your billing cycle.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) as well as ACH bank transfers for annual plans.

How does the Professional plan handle additional users?

The Professional plan includes 5 users. You can add additional users for $15/month each. There's no limit to how many users you can add beyond the included 5.

Is my data secure?

Security is our top priority. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We perform regular backups and maintain SOC 2 compliance.

What if I need help getting started?

We offer onboarding assistance for all new customers. Professional and Enterprise plans include dedicated setup sessions with our customer success team.

Do you offer custom plans?

Yes, we can create custom plans for businesses with unique requirements. Contact our sales team to discuss your specific needs.

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies are widely used by website owners to make their websites work, or to work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information.

We use first and third-party cookies for several reasons. Some cookies are required for technical reasons for our Website to operate, and we refer to these as "essential" or "strictly necessary" cookies. Other cookies enable us to track and target the interests of our users to enhance the experience on our Website.

4. Types of cookies we use

We use the following types of cookies:
